ABOUT SPARKLE

Three Decades of Trusted Fabric Care

Sparkle Dry Cleaners has provided premium fabric care since 1995. With 31+ years of expert experience, we specialize in high-end dry cleaning and laundry services for garments that deserve exceptional care.

From delicate zari sarees and heavy sherwanis to bridal gowns and special occasion wear, our team understands that every fabric requires a different approach.

Our expertise also extends beyond everyday clothing. We provide precision darning (rafu), custom coloring (dyeing), saree fall-bidding and specialized cleaning for leather jackets, shoes, chappals, soft toys, rajais and bulky car covers.
